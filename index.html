About Our Movement

Durdom-25 Blood Donation Society

Founded by dedicated youth and voluntary humanitarians of Jhenaidah, Durdom-25 Blood Donation Society is committed to ensuring that no emergency patient in Jhenaidah suffers due to a shortage of safe blood.

  • 100% Non-profit voluntary blood donor network
  • Instant donor coordination in Jhenaidah Sadar Hospital & clinics
  • Awareness campaigns & free blood grouping camps
